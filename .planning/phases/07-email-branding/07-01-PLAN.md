| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-01 | Injection | renderEmailBase bodyHtml param | mitigate | Consumers MUST sanitize input with DOMPurify before passing to email-base. Documented in JSDoc. email-base itself does NOT sanitize — it is a presentation layer. |
